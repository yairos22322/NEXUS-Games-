# Simulation V3 Playtest Sheet

Use this after pulling the merged V3 build.

## Neon Ops

- `1`, `2`, `3` switch between AR, SMG and DMR.
- Verify each weapon keeps its own ammo when switching away and back.
- Verify switching to a weapon with fewer rounds does not create a tracer or damage destructible cover.
- Hold RMB and verify ADS tightens FOV and accuracy.
- Compare recoil between AR, SMG and DMR.
- Shoot crates/barriers until destroyed and verify enemies can route through the opening afterward.
- Check enemies behind geometry attempt to route rather than continuously pushing into walls.
- At a perk offer use `Z`, `X`, or `C`. Press Escape before choosing and verify gameplay remains frozen behind the overlay.

## Street Rush

- Hold Space while steering at speed to drift.
- Verify pooled skid marks appear and old marks disappear.
- Compare dry vs rain/storm grip.
- Check AI traffic brakes behind slower traffic and sometimes changes lanes.
- Verify player headlights and nitro glow are visible in darker scenes.

## Zombie Siege

- Pull a large horde around ruins and confirm A* assist reduces wall stacking.
- Destroy a runtime crate while zombies are moving around it.
- Verify runners receive stronger knockback than brutes.
- Confirm a perk screen appears after enough nights and pauses the horde.

## Orbital Wars

- Fire rapidly at fast crossing ships and verify projectiles do not visibly tunnel through them.
- Check enemy ships roll/react when damaged.
- Verify engine lighting becomes stronger while boosting.
- Confirm formations and evasive behavior remain active after reactions.

## Cyber Runner

- Verify coyote jump, jump buffering, vault, wall run and wall jump.
- Check landing particles and dash glow.
- In a windy weather preset verify airborne drift is subtle, not disruptive.

## Global

- Pause during active gameplay and verify shared AI stops.
- Watch for frame spikes during large explosions and dense hordes.
- Confirm optional rig detail disappears only at long distance and returns without rapid flicker.
- Confirm contracts progress and reward score/XP/credits.
- Confirm no new errors appear in the terminal.
